The timing that matters: Aug 1–14 is an NCAA shutdown — nobody will answer, so it's drafting week. Send from Aug 15 on. Coaches can't reply directly to you until Sept 1 — but they read everything, and the goal is being a name they already know when the phones open. Send every email from your own address, not a parent's.
What goes in every email
  • Subject: Class of 2028 Attack — [Your Name], HHH Philly / Lake Norman HS — [event you'll be at]
  • Who you are in one line: name, grad year, position, jersey number, club, high school.
  • Your film link, right up top. Coaches decide in 30 seconds whether to keep reading.
  • Your upcoming schedule — especially their camp or a tournament they'll scout.
  • One specific sentence about their program — why them, not a school swap-in.
  • End with a question — questions get replies. Then log it here.
