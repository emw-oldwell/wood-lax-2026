Kate — start here

This is a working document, not a report. Three things to do, in this order:

  1. Set your tiers on the full board. Every school has a 1 · 2 · 3 toggle. Tier 1 started as everyone who put you on their list — move them as you learn things. Nothing here is fixed.
  2. Then work the schedule. Every camp you're eligible for is listed by day underneath. Mark each one Going, Maybe or Skip — the page flags days that collide and keeps a running cost total.

One thing to carry into every camp: ask the staff “how many attackers are you taking in 2028, and what role would I compete for?” A number and a role beat a whole day of vague interest.

September 1, 2026

The date the whole calendar points at

Sept 1 of junior year is the first day D1 coaches can contact you and the first day you can take visits. In the class of 2027, girls' commitments went from about 6 in August to 595 in September — roughly ninety percent of a class goes in the first month. August exists so you're a name with a face attached when the phones open. Also: Aug 1–14 is an NCAA recruiting shutdown (no contact of any kind, so nobody will answer email) and Aug 28–Sep 3 is a dead period.
